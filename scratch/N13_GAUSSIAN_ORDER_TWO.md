# N13: the fixed Gaussian order at two

## What is now formalized

`FLT/Assumptions/MazurProof/N13GaussianOrderTwo.lean` defines the nested
`Z2`-order

```text
G = Z2[i] / (i^2 + 1),
O = G[theta] /
    (theta^3 + 2 theta^2 - theta - 1
      - i (2 theta (theta + 1))).
```

The map

```text
i     |-> 1 + epsilon,
theta |-> alpha
```

to `DualNumber F8` is constructed by two applications of
`AdjoinRoot.lift`.  The defining relations are checked directly.

The main structural results are:

- `pi_sq_eq`: for `pi = 1 - i`, `pi^2 = -2i`;
- `ramifiedSquareIdeal_eq_two`: `(pi^2) = (2)`;
- `reduction_surjective`: the reduction map is onto.  This uses only that
  `F8` is generated by `alpha` and that `pi` maps to `epsilon`;
- `ker_reduction_eq_ramifiedSquareIdeal`: the kernel is exactly `(pi^2)`;
- `orderModRamifiedSquareEquiv`:

  ```text
  O / (pi^2)  ~=  DualNumber F8.
  ```

No finite table or ray-class enumeration occurs.  Kernel exactness is
proved from the two monic power bases `1,i` and `1,theta,theta^2`.
The constant and epsilon components of a zero jet give two polynomials
of degree at most two vanishing at `alpha`.  Since the residue cubic has
degree three, all six binary coefficients vanish.  `PadicInt.ker_toZMod`
then shows that all six `Z2` coefficients are divisible by two.

The short N13 descent generators are also reconstructed inside this
order.  The theorems

```text
reduction_e1Order
reduction_e2Order
reduction_primeAOrder
reduction_primeQOrder
```

show that their images are exactly the jets previously used in
`N13LocalDlogTwo`.

## Remaining arithmetic semantics

The Lean type called `Order` is the explicit nested Gaussian cubic order
above.  It has not yet been identified with the completion of the
maximal order of the global sextic algebra at the chosen prime over two.
Consequently the theorem is currently an exact statement about the
fixed integral presentation, not yet literally a theorem about
`O_{L,P}`.

The missing bridge should establish:

1. a `Q2`-algebra isomorphism from the global N13 sextic algebra (after
   completion at the chosen prime) to the fraction algebra of the
   Gaussian cubic presentation, carrying the global `zeta` and `theta`
   to the displayed `i` and `theta`;
2. that the Gaussian cubic `Z2`-order is the full local integer ring.
   Structurally, this should follow from the ramified quadratic
   extension `Q2(i)/Q2` together with the irreducible separable residue
   cubic `T^3 + T + 1`, which makes the cubic layer unramified;
3. identification of the prime `P` with `(1-i)` in that completed
   integer ring, after which
   `orderModRamifiedSquareEquiv` transports directly to
   `O_{L,P}/P^2`;
4. the separate geometric/Kummer statement that actual local Jacobian
   descent values are represented by the integral or normalized
   coordinate jets from `N13LocalDlogRegimes`.

These are completion, integral-closure, and Kummer-compatibility
questions.  No finite first-jet algebra remains missing.
