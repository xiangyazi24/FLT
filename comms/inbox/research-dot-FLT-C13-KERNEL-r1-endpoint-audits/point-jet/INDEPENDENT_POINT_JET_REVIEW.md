## Polynomial and curve jet maps

The target `TrivSqZeroExt K K` has product `(a,b)*(c,d)=(a*c,a*d+b*c)` for the canonical commutative scalar actions. The candidate's polynomial map sends p to `(p(x),p'(x))`. Evaluation and the Leibniz rule verify its ring-homomorphism fields, with the opposite scalar action correctly converted to ordinary multiplication.

Sending the curve variable y to `(y,s)` maps the good curve equation to a dual number whose first component is

`y²+(x³+x+1)*y-(x⁵+x⁴)`

and whose second component is

`(2*y+x³+x+1)*s+(3*x²+1)*y-(5*x⁴+4*x³)`.

These are exactly the two explicit hypotheses of `root_jet` and `pointJet`. Thus the quotient lift is justified by actual vanishing of the defining polynomial in the dual-number ring. No slope existence, vertical-derivative invertibility, smoothness, or injectivity claim is silently assumed. The construction works over any field, including positive characteristic, because the formal derivative and numeric coefficients are interpreted in that field.

The xClass and yClass formulas are the exact AdjoinRoot lift-of-coefficient and lift-root APIs. They return the intended polynomial value/derivative pair and point/tangent pair, respectively.

## Squared graph ideal detection

Let J be the actual generalized graph ideal generated by xClass(u) and yClass-xClass(v). The first-component projection of pointJet is a ring map to K. The two visible hypotheses u(x)=0 and v(x)=y show each generator lies in its kernel, so every element of J has jet with zero first component. This argument does not require v'(x)=s: it only claims vanishing of the value on the graph ideal.

If a,b∈J, their jets have the forms `(0,a')` and `(0,b')`, whose product is zero in the square-zero extension. `Ideal.mul_le.mpr` then extends this product calculation to the full ideal product J*J=J² and the kernel of pointJet. The proof therefore handles sums of products in the squared ideal; it does not assume that every member is a single square or a single product.

The relevant pinned API is `Ideal.mul_le : I*J≤K ↔ ∀a∈I,∀b∈J,a*b∈K`. This use is direct and does not involve the oppositely named left/right product-inclusion lemmas that required a correction in the separate good-shape module.

Applying the zero-jet result to `p(x)+q(x)*y` and extracting both coordinates yields exactly

* value: p(x)+q(x)*y=0
* derivative: p'(x)+q'(x)*y+q(x)*s=0

The derivative multiplication order differs from the square-zero representation only by commutativity, handled by the final ring calculation. This is one-way detection from squared graph-ideal membership. The candidate does not assert that the jet map is injective, faithful, or has kernel exactly J², nor that value/derivative vanishing implies graph-square membership.
