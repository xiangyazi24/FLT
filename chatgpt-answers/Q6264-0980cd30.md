# The exact missing geometric bridge

The current fullClosedPointGrading25Two is indexed by exact arithmetic-Frobenius orbits. The affine Dedekind layer is indexed by

```javascript
IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum (ChartQuotient i).
```

Those are mathematically the same closed points inside an affine chart, but there is no current pinned theorem identifying the two representations.

The bridge needs two pieces, not one:

1. point/prime identification — the exact-Frobenius orbit gives the kernel of evaluation on a chart;

1. residue-degree compatibility — the custom atomDegree equals the degree of the resulting finite residue field over ZMod 2.

The second part is essential. Without it, even a valid local order cannot be inserted honestly into the project's weighted divisorDegree.

Pinned Mathlib does not currently provide the first theorem.

FractionalIdeal.finprod_heightOneSpectrum_factorization_principal only says that a principal fractional ideal factors into affine height-one primes with the expected exponents. On a nonproper affine chart the sum of weighted exponents need not be zero: the missing contribution is at the boundary. Consequently one cannot derive global degree zero by summing FractionalIdeal.count on one chart.

The honest proof options after the local bridge are:

1. Function-field product formula. Regard K/F₂(t) as a one-variable function field, compare all normalized valuations and residue degrees, and prove the usual global product formula. Mathlib has substantial function-field and Dedekind algebra, but I found no turnkey product-formula theorem at this pin.

1. N25 projective homogeneous route. Represent f as a ratio of homogeneous elements of equal degree in the Proj function field and prove zeros and poles have equal total intersection degree. This would use the specific projective complete-intersection geometry, but the current Artin calculations only certify one particular hyperplane, not arbitrary homogeneous sections; it is therefore not yet shorter.

1. Divisor/line-bundle degree route. Prove that multiplication by f identifies the corresponding invertible sheaves and degree is invariant under isomorphism. The project does not yet have the general divisor-to-line-bundle/degree API needed to make this shorter.

So n25PrincipalDivisor_degree_zero should remain a named theorem frontier. Do not manufacture it by putting a degree-zero proof into the definition of n25Principal.

Therefore these vector-space lengths must not be fed into FractionalIdeal.count as if they were already orders of vanishing, and they certainly cannot prove the global product formula.

They remain highly useful later: once the chart-prime/stalk bridge exists, they can certify the coefficients of the specific hyperplane divisor at the three F₂-rational points.

# Role of Ω_C ≅ O_C(1)

It does not provide orders of arbitrary rational functions, finite support, or the product formula.

# The first genuinely missing mathematical lemma

If one ignores the tiny pivot helper, the first genuinely new mathematical statement is not the product formula. It is the Frobenius-invariant chart-prime bridge:

```javascript
theorem chartQuotientEval_frobenius_ker
    (d : ℕ) (hd : 0 < d)
    (i : Fin 4)
    (P : CurvePointOnChart i (CommonField 2 d)) :
    RingHom.ker (chartQuotientEval i P) =
      RingHom.ker
        (chartQuotientEval i
          ⟨pointFrobenius canonicalTwoModel 2 d P.1,
            by simpa using normalizedPivot_pointFrobenius
              canonicalTwoModel 2 d P.1⟩)
```

Everything in its proof is already available algebraically: coordinatewise Frobenius, MvPolynomial evaluation, and injectivity of the field automorphism. What is missing is merely the N25-specific quotient-evaluation map and the explicit statement that evaluation commutes with Frobenius.

After that theorem, quotienting by exact Frobenius orbits to obtain a well-defined chart prime is a structural Lean exercise rather than another global theorem.

# Final recommendation

Implement the canonical-pivot → chart-evaluation-kernel chain next. It is the narrow bridge that lets the newly completed full Frobenius grading and the newly completed Dedekind affine divisor layer actually talk to each other.

Do not start by proving a global degree-zero theorem from the Artin lengths or from Ω_C ≅ O_C(1). Those facts are valuable for the canonical hyperplane, but they do not establish the product formula for arbitrary rational functions.

Once fullClosedPointChartPrime, its injectivity, and its residue-degree theorem compile, the construction of the finitely supported global principal divisor becomes mostly existing FractionalIdeal.count/finite_factors algebra.
