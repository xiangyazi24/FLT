The cusp action is

\[
	au(G)=4G,\qquad \lambda(G)=(1-	au)G=-3G.

\[

\[

\[

\[

All identities except the divisor statements are direct `field_simp` followed by `ring_nf`.  The divisor statements follow from ramification index \(3\) at each \(P_i\) and the displayed cyclic-cover equation.
