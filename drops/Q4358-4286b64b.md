# N = 18: complete λ = 1 − ζ₃ Selmer certificate

## Verdict

The λ-Selmer group is exactly the unavoidable torsion line:

$$
Sel^λ(J/Q) = ⟨δ(G)⟩ ≅ Z/3Z,
$$

with

$$
δ(G) = [(-2, ζ²)] = [(2, ζ²)].
$$

Here ζ = ζ₃. After restriction to F = Q(ζ), the complete list of globally admissible, conjugation-equivariant Kummer classes is

$$
(1,1),  (2,ζ²),  (4,ζ).
$$

They are the three powers of the class of the rational cusp divisor G. There is no surviving positive-rank class. Consequently

$$
rank_{Z[ζ]} J(Q) = 0,
$$

$$
rank_Z J(Q) = 0,
$$

and the same computation gives

$$
Sha(J/Q)[λ] = 0.
$$

Thus Route B closes the rank computation using one degree-9 Eisenstein isogeny descent. It does not require genus-2 Chabauty or the generic sextic 2-descent.

# 1. The cyclic cubic cover in descent form

Put

$$
F = Q(ζ),  ζ² + ζ + 1 = 0,
$$

$$
π = 1 − ζ,  s = 1 + 2ζ = ζπ.
$$

Then

$$
s² = −3,  π² = −3ζ.
$$

On

$$
C: y² = f(x),
$$

where

$$
f(x) = x⁶ + 4x⁵ + 10x⁴ + 10x³ + 5x² + 2x + 1,
$$

let

$$
τ(x,y) = (−1 − 1/x, y/x³).
$$

The identity

$$
x⁶ f(−1 − 1/x) = f(x)
$$

and a second substitution give τ³ = 1.

Define

$$
d = x(x+1),  n = x³ − 3x − 1,
$$

$$
t = n/d,  v = y/d.
$$

The exact polynomial identity

$$
f = n² + 4nd + 12d²
$$

gives the quotient conic

$$
v² = t² + 4t + 12.
$$

Parametrize it through (−1,3) by

$$
m = (v−3)/(t+1).
$$

Then

$$
t = (−m² − 6m + 3)/(m² − 1),
$$

$$
v = (−3m² + 2m − 3)/(m² − 1).
$$

In the original coordinates,

$$
m = (y − 3d)/(n+d).
$$

Let

$$
x₀ = x,  x₁ = τ(x),  x₂ = τ²(x).
$$

These are the three roots of

$$
Z³ − tZ² − (t+3)Z − 1.
$$

Its discriminant is

$$
(t² + 3t + 9)².
$$

The Lagrange resolvent

$$
R = x₀ + ζ²x₁ + ζx₂
$$

satisfies

$$
τ(R) = ζR,
$$

$$
R³ = (t−3ζ)(t−3ζ²)².
$$

Set w = (m²−1)R. Define

$$
γ₁ = (3+2s)/7 = (5+4ζ)/7,
$$

$$
γ₂ = s = 1+2ζ,
$$

$$
γ₃ = (3−2s)/7 = (1−4ζ)/7,
$$

$$
γ₄ = −s = −1−2ζ,
$$

and

$$
c₀ = −(1+3ζ)(1+3ζ²)².
$$

The two exact factorizations are

$$
t−3ζ = −(1+3ζ)(m−γ₁)(m−γ₂)/(m²−1),
$$

$$
t−3ζ² = −(1+3ζ²)(m−γ₃)(m−γ₄)/(m²−1).
$$

Therefore C over F is birational to

$$
w³ = c₀ (m−γ₁)(m−γ₂)(m−γ₃)²(m−γ₄)².  
$$

All identities in this section become polynomial identities after clearing the displayed denominators and are directly checkable by `field_simp` followed by `ring_nf`.

# 2. Two branch divisors and two true Kummer functions

Let Pᵢ = (m,w) = (γᵢ,0). The Pᵢ are F-rational and fixed by the deck transformation τ(w) = ζw.

Put

$$
D₁ = [P₁−P₄],  D₂ = [P₂−P₄],  D₃ = [P₃−P₄].
$$

The cyclic-cover equation gives

$$
div(w/(m−γ₄)²) = D₁ + D₂ + 2D₃.
$$

Hence

$$
D₃ = D₁ + D₂.
$$

For a cyclic cubic cover with four ramification points, the branch subgroup fixed by τ has dimension 4−2 = 2 over F₃. Thus

$$
J[λ] = ⟨D₁,D₂⟩ ≅ (Z/3Z)²,
$$

where λ = 1−τ.

The dual isogeny is

$$
λ̂ = 1−τ² = −τ²(1−τ),
$$

so ker λ̂ = ker λ. The following functions therefore give the true λ-Kummer map rather than merely a fake quotient:

$$
h₁ = (m−γ₁)/(m−γ₄),
$$

$$
h₂ = (m−γ₂)/(m−γ₄).
$$

Their divisors are

$$
div(h₁) = 3D₁,  div(h₂) = 3D₂.
$$

In the original x,y coordinates,

$$
hᵢ = (y−3d−γᵢ(n+d))/(y−3d−γ₄(n+d)).
$$

For a degree-zero divisor D = Σ n_Q Q, disjoint from the relevant supports, define

$$
δ_F(D) = ( Π h₁(Q)^{n_Q},  Π h₂(Q)^{n_Q} )
$$

as a class in

$$
(Fˣ/Fˣ³)².
$$

For the local point computations below, use the rational cusp over m₀ = 1 as base point:

$$
κ_v(m) = (h₁(m)/h₁(1), h₂(m)/h₂(1)).
$$

## Complex conjugation

Complex conjugation exchanges

$$
γ₁ ↔ γ₃,  γ₂ ↔ γ₄.
$$

Geometrically,

$$
D̄₁ = D₁,  D̄₂ = −D₂.
$$

At the level of functions,

$$
h̄₂ = h₂^{-1},
$$

while the cyclic-cover equation gives the exact cube relation

$$
h̄₁/h₁ = c₀^{-2}
  ( w² / ((m−γ₁)(m−γ₂)(m−γ₃)(m−γ₄)) )³.
$$

The constant c₀^{-2} evaluates trivially on a degree-zero divisor. Therefore conjugation acts on Kummer classes by

$$
ι(α,β) = (ᾱ, β̄^{-1}).
$$

This is the required geometric relation and equivariance.

# 3. The finite global cube-class space

The only relevant finite primes are

$$
S = { p₂ = (2), p₃ = (π) }.
$$

The prime 2 is inert, 3 is ramified, F has class number 1, and

$$
O_Fˣ = μ₆.
$$

Since −1 is itself a cube,

$$
F(S,3) = ⟨ζ,2,π⟩ / Fˣ³ ≅ F₃³.
$$

Before imposing conjugation, the true Kummer search space is

$$
(F(S,3))² ≅ F₃⁶,
$$

with 3⁶ = 729 raw classes.

Write

$$
α = ζ^{a₁} 2^{b₁} π^{c₁},
$$

$$
β = ζ^{a₂} 2^{b₂} π^{c₂}.
$$

Modulo cubes,

$$
ζ̄ = ζ²,  π̄ = −ζ²π.
$$

Using the conjugation action above, invariance is equivalent to

$$
a₁ = c₁,  b₂ = 0,  c₂ = 0,
$$

with a₂, b₁, c₁ arbitrary.

Put

$$
a = a₂,  b = b₁,  c = c₁.
$$

Since s = ζπ, the 729 raw classes reduce to the exact list of 27 Q-classes

$$
(α,β) = (2^b s^c, ζ^a),
$$

where

$$
(a,b,c) ∈ F₃³.
$$

Because [F:Q] = 2 is prime to 3, restriction-corestriction kills the higher cohomology of Gal(F/Q) on the relevant 3-primary modules. Globally and locally, the Q-Kummer image is therefore exactly the conjugation-invariant part of the F-Kummer image. No extra obstruction is introduced at this step.

# 6. Local image at the ramified prime p₃

Let

$$
K₃ = F_{p₃} = Q₃(ζ),  π = 1−ζ.
$$

The element π satisfies the Eisenstein polynomial

$$
π² − 3π + 3 = 0.
$$

Put

$$
u₂ = 1+π²,  u₃ = 1+π³.
$$

The unit filtration gives

$$
U₁ = μ₃ × U₂,  U₂³ = U₄.
$$

Consequently

$$
K₃ˣ/K₃ˣ³ = ⟨π,ζ,u₂,u₃⟩ ≅ F₃⁴.
$$

Use coordinate order

```text
[alpha_pi, alpha_zeta, alpha_u2, alpha_u3 |
 beta_pi,  beta_zeta,  beta_u2,  beta_u3].
```

The finite class identities needed later are

$$
[2] = (0,0,1,1),
$$

$$
[ζ] = (0,1,0,0),
$$

$$
[s] = [ζπ] = (1,1,0,0).
$$

For the first identity, −1 is a cube and

$$
−2 = (1+π²)(1+π³)  mod U₄.
$$

The remaining quotient lies in U₄ = U₂³.

Here v_π(3) = 2, so

$$
|3|_{p₃} = 3^{-2} = 1/9.

The local isogeny-index formula gives

$$
#(J(K₃)/λJ(K₃)) = 9 × |3|_{p₃}^{-1} = 81.

The four displayed vectors are independent, hence already span 3⁴ = 81 classes. Therefore the matrix and parameterization above are the full local image.

Using the local class identities,

$$
loc₃(a,b,c) = (c,c,b,b | 0,a,0,0).
$$

Compare this with the local image parameterization:

1. The first coordinate forces c = 0.
2. Then r = 0, q = b, and u = b.
3. The beta-zeta coordinate forces a = 2b.

Thus the p₃ condition is exactly

$$

$$

# 8. Rank and Sha

The cusp action is

$$
τ(G) = 4G,
$$

so

$$
λ(G) = (1−τ)G = −3G.
$$

Hence

$$
J(Q)_tors / λJ(Q)_tors ≅ F₃.
$$

Let

$$
r = rank_{Z[ζ]} J(Q).
$$

The torsion-free quotient is free over the PID Z[ζ], so

$$
dim_{F₃}(J(Q)/λJ(Q)) = r+1.
$$

The Kummer exact sequence is

$$
0 → J(Q)/λJ(Q) → Sel^λ(J/Q) → Sha(J/Q)[λ] → 0.
$$

The Selmer group has dimension 1, while torsion already contributes dimension 1. Therefore

$$
r = 0,
$$

$$
Sha(J/Q)[λ] = 0,
$$

and

$$
rank_Z J(Q) = 2r = 0.
$$
